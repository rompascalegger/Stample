HOW TO PLAY

  1. Smash the rock. Tap it to swing your pickaxe, or hold to keep swinging. It wiggles: a swing that misses does nothing.
  2. Damage = (strength × 10 + pickaxe × 100) ÷ hardness, give or take 10%. Some swings are critical: the brighter the number, the harder the hit.
  3. Combo: every hit in a row adds 4% damage (up to 3× at 50). A miss or a pause of 1.5 s breaks it. Every 10 is a milestone.
  4. Geodes ✸ glitter in the dark (about 1 rock in 25): double health, but triple score, three lucky finds and a pile of gold.
  5. Every rock is different. Most are soft, a few are rock-hard. Hard rocks wiggle slower, so they're easier to hit.
  6. Each broken rock pays score, a few gold coins and one find: ◇ stone, ◆ mineral, ✦ gem, ❖ precious gem or ✸ element. Stones and minerals come in small stacks. A lucky rock glows and drops better finds.
  7. ⚱ Fossils: about 1 rock in 100 has a bone showing through it. Dig it out for a dinosaur, one of 100 in the ARCHEOLOGY collection, plus 5× score and a heap of gold. Dinosaurs come from nowhere else.
  8. Style points: a dead-center swing is a PERFECT hit (2×). Overkill a rock for +25% score, break one fast for extra gold, and a 50 combo starts 10 seconds of GOLD FEVER.
  9. The mine has moods: cave-ins, a canary to catch, gold veins and the occasional ghost. Rocks talk back. Some have googly eyes. And there are secrets.
  10. 🍄 Mushrooms: about 1 rock in 200 is a glowing mushroom growing out of the stone. Pick it for one of 100 real species in the MUSHROOM collection, plus 8× score, a pile of gold and a few very strange seconds.
  11. Reach the goal before you run out of rocks and the cage takes you one level deeper (50 m down), with more rocks: 6 + 3 × level. Every level's goal is 15% higher than the last.
  12. Deeper is darker. Your lamp reaches less far, the mine sounds lower and stranger, and more of them open their eyes to watch you dig.
  13. Out of rocks below the goal = your lamp goes out and the run is over. Your score is everything you mined this run.
  14. Finds stay forever. Sell them in the 🎒 BAG for gold, or keep them: the 🛒 SHOP wants gold and specific finds for every upgrade.
  15. Upgrades: strength, agility (steadier hits), luck (better finds and score), pickaxe, insight (better finds, but rocks get tougher and wilder), dimension (more score, tougher rocks), slow motion (calmer rock) and player power (more score).
  16. ⛏ Auto-mine swings at the rock on its own, 2.5 times a second and without a combo. Mining by hand is much faster. The ≋ button silences the mine's background sounds; ♪ silences everything.
  17. Falling nuggets drift by now and then. Tap them for a few points; a rare golden one is worth gold.
