How to play

  1. Postcards arrive from a chain of pen pals, one in every country of the world. Each card goes to the next country, and its pen pal writes the next card.
  2. Peel the stamp slowly. Drag across it again and again. Fast drags build up strain; at full strain the stamp tears. Old stamps are more fragile.
  3. The stamp comes from the card's country, and its release year is real. Its id is the country code and that year, like GB-1840.
  4. The seal (postmark) comes from a random country, in a year between the stamp's release and 2026.
  5. Quality (out of 100):
    • Condition: no tears 40, one tear 28, two 16, more 6
    • Luck: the closer the seal is to the stamp's center, the more of 25
    • Home seal: seal from the stamp's own country +20
    • First day: seal year is the stamp's release year +15
  6. The album keeps the best copy of every stamp. Sort it by origin, by year, or by seal quality. Stamps you don't have yet are just ?.
